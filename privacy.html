Owlveria Uploader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026.

Owlveria Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Owlveria YouTube channels to upload and schedule the owner's own videos on those channels.

It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and to read the owner's own channel name, so that each upload goes to the right channel. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.

The app does not collect, store, sell or share data about anyone else. It does not use analytics or advertising, and it has no other users.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Questions: through the Owlveria YouTube channel.
